### Edits made to the supplied wording

- “pistolveida” is written “pistoļveida”, as in other Latvian firearm texts. The step ID is unchanged, so scoring is unaffected.
- Earlier display edits are kept: `Salocīt laidi`, `aptveres ligzda` (instead of “magazīna”), `pastobrs`, `aizslēga atgriezējmehānisms` (from “Aizslēgatgriezējmehānismu”).

### Points for the instructor

1. **Order.** The manual removes the magazine well before folding the stock and does not unscrew the flash hider during a field strip. The course order is mechanically possible; confirm that it is the intended one.
2. **Unscored stages.** The learning mode adds the manual's safety check (remove the magazine, check the chamber) before disassembly and the function check after assembly. They are not part of the test.
3. **Pictures.** The scene is built from a field-strip photograph of a G36C that appears to be a replica with a two-piece receiver. The middle of the receiver is rebuilt from its own texture, and the pins, firing pin, gas piston, operating rod and barrel section are renders. The movements are correct; small details of the replica may differ from the service rifle.

## AK-4

No course order was supplied, so the AK-4 module follows the published Latvian procedure: **Jaunsarga rokasgrāmata**, chapter 12.2 “Triecienšautenes daļējā izjaukšana” (based on “Triecienšautene AK-4 (G-3)”, Rīga 2005). The step labels are the answer key of the AK-4 order test:

1. Atvienot laidi ar atgriezējmehānismu
2. Izņemt aizslēga rāmi ar aizslēgu
3. Atvienot pistoļveida rokturi ar šaušanas mehānismu
4. Noņemt pastobri
5. Izņemt drošinātāju-pārslēdzēju
6. Izņemt šaušanas mehānismu
7. Atvienot aizslēgu un belžņa ieliktni
8. Izņemt belzni un belžņa atsperi

The handbook's step 6 (“Atvienot šaušanas mehānismu no korpusa”) is split into steps 5 and 6, in the order the handbook describes the actions. The unscored safety check (magazine out, chamber check, control press) comes first, the function check (S, P, A) after assembly. How each part moves – pins pushed out sideways, stock straight back, the cocking handle pushing the bolt carrier out of the rear, the grip down, the handguard's front down and then forward, the selector turned anticlockwise to vertical, the trigger mechanism lifted by its hammer, the bolt turned and pulled off with the locking piece – is confirmed in the Bundeswehr manual ZDv 3/13 “Das Gewehr G3”, Nr. 319–323 (the AK-4 is the Swedish licence build of the G3).

### Points for the instructor

1. **Sources differ.** A Jaunsardze unit handout for the AK-4 (republished on spoki.lv) releases the grip before removing the bolt carrier, has an extra step “novieto stobru ar stobra kārbu” and tells the trainee *not* to do the control press. The module follows the handbook, which does the control press and has no such steps. The German manual also unhooks the sling and unscrews the flash hider; neither is part of the Latvian procedure, so neither is animated.
2. **Where the pins go.** The Latvian texts say only “izbīdīt uz sāniem” and “ievietot pagaidu vietā”. The animation follows the German manual: the two stock pins go into the hollow rivets near the butt, the grip and handguard pins back into their holes. The pins come out on the left side (their heads are on the left, as on other HK rifles); confirm on the course rifles.
3. **Selector markings.** The function check uses S, P and A as in the Latvian material; the photographed rifle is a German G3A3 with S, E, F. Other small details of the Swedish AK-4 (sights, markings) may differ from the photos.
4. **Pictures.** The parts come from a Wikimedia Commons collage of a G3A3 whose parts were photographed at different scales and some from the other side. They are rotated, mirrored (stock, grip) and scaled to match the picture of the assembled rifle in the same photo; the bolt carrier is a top view rebuilt as a side view, the magazine is stretched to side-view proportions, and the sizes of the small bolt parts are estimates. The movements are right; exact proportions inside the receiver are approximate.
5. **Technical data.** The theory questions use the handbook's values (1045 mm, 450 mm barrel, 4,5 kg, 20 rounds, 400 m, 500–650 rounds/min, 780–800 m/s, 4 grooves to the right, sight 200–500 m). The German manual gives slightly different figures for the G3 (e.g. 4,40 kg, sight 200–400 m, maximum range 3700 m instead of 4500 m) and the unit handout 4,7 kg; the questions avoid the figures the sources disagree on most (maximum range) but the mass and sight range should be confirmed for the course rifles.

## Theory questions (both weapons)

Each weapon now has a “Tehniskie dati un darbība” topic in the tests: 10 questions for the G36C from the Heckler & Koch G36 technical data (G36C column: 5,56 × 45 mm, 30 rounds, gas operation with a rotating bolt, 228 mm barrel, 716 / 500 mm, about 750 rounds/min and 750 m/s, about 2988 g) and 16 for the AK-4 from the sources above. Wrong answers use the other weapon's figures where they make good distractors (e.g. the G36's gas operation for the AK-4 and the other way round).

## Drill question bank (Ierinda)

The 100 questions are used as supplied (`src/drill/ierinda-bank.json`). Only the typography is adjusted when they are shown: the em dashes (—) are displayed as the Latvian en dash (–).

1. **Answer length gives the answer away.** In 71 of the 100 questions the correct answer is the longest option, and in 40 it is more than 1.6 times longer than the longest wrong answer: 01-04, 01-08, 02-02, 02-05, 02-08, 03-01, 03-02, 03-04, 03-06, 03-07, 03-09, 04-04, 04-06, 04-07, 04-08, 05-01, 05-02, 05-03, 05-04, 05-06, 05-09, 06-06, 06-10, 07-02, 07-03, 07-05, 07-07, 07-08, 07-09, 07-10, 08-05, 08-09, 08-10, 09-01, 09-02, 09-05, 09-07, 09-08, 10-01, 10-10. Always choosing the longest answer scores about 71 %, which passes at the 70 % pass mark. Lengthening the wrong answers (or shortening the correct ones) of these questions is recommended.
2. **Near-duplicates.** 01-09 / 02-01 (weapon safety check before drill), 06-07 / 06-08 (mirror images of each other) and 06-05 / 10-04 (“ATLIKT!”) ask almost the same thing. Chapter 10 repeats earlier chapters on purpose, so this may be intended.
3. **Wording to check.** “sānis” in the wrong answers of 05-03 and 07-07 (standard form: “sāņus”); 02-10 “saņemtas pavēles” reads more naturally as “saņemtās pavēles”; 08-05 uses “skaitīšanās”, 08-09 “saskaitīšanās” for the same procedure.

## Generated weapon questions (combined test)

The combined test adds questions generated from the weapon module content: the function of each part, naming a part from its photo, the direction a part moves during disassembly and which step follows another (plus the theory questions above). Wrong answers are taken from the other steps of the same weapon; parts that look alike in a photo (the pins) are never offered as each other's wrong answer. Steps whose movement is an action rather than a direction (unhook, unscrew) get no direction question.
